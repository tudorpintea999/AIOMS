# AIOMS

**Next-generation order management systems with AI integrated.**

AIOMS is a modern order management platform that puts AI at the core of how orders are captured, processed, and fulfilled. Instead of bolting automation onto legacy workflows, AIOMS is built from the ground up so intelligent agents can handle the repetitive work while people stay in control of the decisions that matter.

## Vision

Order management should be simple for every business, not only for companies with large IT teams. AIOMS aims to make agentic AI practical and approachable for non-technical operators.

## Planned capabilities

- **AI order intake** from email, chat, and messaging channels, parsed into structured orders automatically
- **Smart validation** that flags missing details, pricing errors, and duplicates before they cause problems
- **Inventory awareness** with real-time stock checks and proactive low-stock alerts
- **Automated status updates** sent to customers at every stage of fulfillment
- **Natural-language insights** so you can ask questions like "What were our top products last month?"
- **Human-in-the-loop controls** for approvals, overrides, and exceptions

## Status

Early stage. Architecture and roadmap in progress.
